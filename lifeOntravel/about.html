Our Story

Born in the Wild,
Built for Explorers

Life On Travel was founded in 2009 by a group of naturalists and conservationists who believed that the best way to protect India's wilderness is to share it with those who truly care. Over fifteen years, we have grown from a small team of three into one of the country's most respected wildlife travel companies — never losing sight of why we started.

Every safari we lead is a conversation between human and wild. We don't chase numbers; we craft moments.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Everything we do flows from three unshakeable principles that have guided us since the very first safari.

🌿

Conservation First

A portion of every booking goes directly to habitat restoration and anti-poaching initiatives. We are members of the Wildlife Conservation Trust and operate under strict ethical guidelines that protect the ecosystems we visit.

🔭

Expert-Led Immersion

Every guide in our network holds a degree in ecology or zoology, with an average of 12 years field experience. We believe depth of knowledge transforms a sighting into a story you'll tell for the rest of your life.

✨

Crafted for You

No two guests are the same. Whether you are a wildlife photographer, a family seeking adventure, or a couple escaping into silence — we design every itinerary with your specific rhythms, interests, and pace in mind.

How We Got Here

The Life On Travel Journey

2009

Three naturalists from Jim Corbett launch Life On Travel with a single jeep and an unshakeable belief that wildlife experiences should be ethical, immersive, and extraordinary.

●
●
2013

Expansion to Ranthambore and Kaziranga. Our first international guests arrive from the UK and Germany — word-of-mouth has reached beyond India's borders.

2016

Launch of the Conservation Partnership Program. We begin channeling 5% of all revenues directly into habitat protection with the Wildlife Conservation Trust.

●
●
2019

Recognized as one of India's Top 10 Sustainable Travel Companies by Outlook Traveller. Our fleet grows to 24 vehicles across 12 reserves.

2024

Over 1,200 safaris led. A 98% guest satisfaction rate. The same passion that started it all — now shared by a team of 60 naturalists, guides, and conservationists across India.
